// Features

Everything included.
Nothing gated.

📅

Campaigns & scheduling

Pick a template, tone, language, and cadence. TootFlow spaces posts across your campaign duration — pause, resume, and reschedule any time.

🤖

AI drafts, your control

AI generates the draft schedule; you edit every word. Per-post refinement with natural-language instructions. 10 templates, 12 tones, 3 languages.

🐘

Proper Mastodon support

Polls on any post, multiple instances, multi-account linking — built the Fediverse way, not as an afterthought.

⏰

Set-and-forget publishing

A background scheduler checks every minute and publishes due posts. Transient errors retry automatically; permanent ones fail visibly.

📊

Engagement metrics

Favourites, boosts, replies, and follower snapshots — tracked per post with weekly charts, so you know what actually landed.

📤

Export Studio

Turn a finished campaign into a newsletter digest, a LinkedIn article, or a Reddit post. Your content, second life included.
