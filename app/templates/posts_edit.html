Craft Tips
One idea per toot Don't cram multiple topics. Focus drives engagement.
Use your 500 chars Get to the point fast. Cut anything that doesn't add value.
End with a hook A question or call-to-action drives replies and reach.
Line breaks matter White space makes toots scannable. Break at natural pauses.
2–3 hashtags max Pick specific ones. More looks spammy and dilutes focus.
Vary your format Mix statements, questions, and short threads across your campaign.
Links at the end URLs eat chars and disrupt reading flow — put them last.
Read it aloud If it sounds robotic, rewrite it. Real voice outperforms polish.
Polls drive replies A simple 2-option poll can double engagement. Keep options short and the question obvious.
Poll timing 1-day polls create urgency. 7-day polls suit slow-burn topics or weekly check-ins.
