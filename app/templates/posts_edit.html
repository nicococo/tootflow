Craft Tips
One idea per tootDon't cram multiple topics. Focus drives engagement.
Use your 500 charsGet to the point fast. Cut anything that doesn't add value.
End with a hookA question or call-to-action drives replies and reach.
Line breaks matterWhite space makes toots scannable. Break at natural pauses.
2–3 hashtags maxPick specific ones. More looks spammy and dilutes focus.
Vary your formatMix statements, questions, and short threads across your campaign.
Links at the endURLs eat chars and disrupt reading flow — put them last.
Read it aloudIf it sounds robotic, rewrite it. Real voice outperforms polish.
Polls drive repliesA simple 2-option poll can double engagement.
Poll timing1-day polls create urgency. 7-day polls suit slow-burn topics.
